Helium helps you launch paywall experiments in minutes to boost in-app revenue, and lets eligible users pay on the web instead of through the App Store.

## Two ways to charge, one paywall

Every Helium paywall sells your App Store or Google Play products. On iOS in the US, the same paywall can also offer a **web product**: the user taps it, Helium opens a hosted web paywall in the browser, the purchase completes with Paddle, and the user returns to your app with access unlocked. Paying through the web means processor fees instead of the store's 15–30% commission.

You don't choose between the two. Users outside the eligible audience (Android, non-US storefronts) see the in-app purchase path automatically, and eligible users can always **continue in-app** instead. The [Quickstart](/getting-started/quickstart) sets up both; [Web checkout (App2Web)](/web-checkout/overview) explains how the web path works.
