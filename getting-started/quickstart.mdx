This quickstart takes you from a new account to a live, AI-editable paywall in your app that sells your store products and, for eligible users, offers [App2Web checkout](/web-checkout/overview) on the web.

<Info>
  App2Web is available on iOS for US storefronts and uses Paddle as the payment provider. Everyone else automatically gets the normal in-app purchase path from the same paywall, so you can follow every step below on any platform; the web-specific parts simply don't apply to Android.
</Info>

<Steps>
  <Step title="Create your account">
    Create your Helium account at [app.tryhelium.com](https://app.tryhelium.com). This takes you to the **Helium dashboard**, where you create paywalls, manage API keys and integrations, run A/B tests and view analytics.
  </Step>

  <Step title="Connect your products">
    **Store products.** Visit [Integrations → Products](https://app.tryhelium.com/integrations/products) and sync your products from the App Store and/or Google Play.

    **Web products.** Create your products and prices in Paddle, then connect Paddle under **Settings → Integrations → Paddle** and click **Sync Products**. Paddle prices appear alongside your store products and can be added to any paywall. Steps 1–3 of the [Paddle onboarding guide](/guides/paddle-onboarding-guide) cover the Paddle account, payment link and connection.
  </Step>

  <Step title="Build your paywall">
    Go to [Paywalls](https://app.tryhelium.com/paywalls), click **New Paywall** and build it from a template, a screenshot or scratch; see [Using the paywall editor](/guides/makingpaywalls).

    Add your store products. To offer web checkout, also add a Paddle product with a **purchase** action, keep a **Continue in-app** path to the store products, and create and link a **hosted web paywall** that opens in the browser. [Build an App2Web paywall](/guides/build-app2web-paywall) walks through the layout.

    <Note>
      You can swap products in and out later. Just make sure the products are linked in the paywall before going live.
    </Note>
  </Step>

  <Step title="Create a workflow and trigger">
    Once you have published a paywall, visit [Workflows](https://app.tryhelium.com/workflows) to create your first workflow, then add a **trigger**.

    - A **trigger** is the string your app passes to Helium to say "show the paywall for this moment" (for example `onboarding` or `premium_feature`). Create one per place a paywall can appear.
    - A **workflow** maps triggers to paywalls, with targeting and experiments.

    Target the paywall with web checkout to the **App2Web eligibility** audience preset; route everyone else to an in-app-only paywall. The hosted web paywall itself is never added to a workflow; it opens only from the in-app paywall.
  </Step>

  <Step title="Install and set up the SDK">
    Pick your platform. Each quickstart covers install, initialize, present a paywall for a trigger, and the **Web checkout (App2Web)** section, which must be done before `initialize` (enable web checkout, set the user ID, handle the return deep link).

    <CardGroup cols={2}>
      <Card title="iOS" icon="apple" href="/sdk/quickstart-ios">
        Swift/SwiftUI/UIKit
      </Card>
      <Card title="Android" icon="android" href="/sdk/quickstart-android">
        Kotlin/Jetpack Compose
      </Card>
      <Card title="React Native" icon="react" href="/sdk/quickstart-react-native">
        React Native/Expo
      </Card>
      <Card title="Flutter" icon="flutter" href="/sdk/quickstart-flutter">
        Flutter integration
      </Card>
    </CardGroup>
  </Step>

  <Step title="Connect revenue and entitlements">
    **Store revenue.** Follow [Set up revenue tracking](/guides/revenue-reporting) to forward App Store Server Notifications, Google Play RTDN or RevenueCat webhooks to Helium.

    **Web revenue and access.** Paddle webhooks are configured for you when you connect Paddle. In your app, unlock paid features when either the store or the web entitlement is active, e.g. `Helium.entitlements.hasActivePaddleEntitlement()` on iOS. If RevenueCat or your own backend is the source of truth, see [Configure your entitlements](/guides/paddle-onboarding-guide#6-configure-your-entitlements).

    <Tip>
      Revenue reporting is what powers subscription, revenue and experiment metrics in the dashboard. It's a _must_ to get the most out of Helium.
    </Tip>
  </Step>

  <Step title="Set up fallbacks">
    Follow the [fallbacks guide](/guides/fallback-bundle) so a paywall shows even when the device is offline on first launch.
  </Step>

  <Step title="Test on device">
    Triple-tap any Helium paywall in a test build to open [Paywall previews](/guides/paywall-previews) and preview any paywall, including unpublished ones, without meeting targeting. Previewing an App2Web paywall runs the full browser checkout with a [simulated or real purchase](/guides/app2web-previews).

    Then run through the [QA guide](/guides/helium-qa-guide) with a real trigger on a physical device.
  </Step>

  <Step title="Go live">
    - [ ] Paywall created with store products (and web product + linked web paywall, if using App2Web)
    - [ ] Workflow + trigger created, App2Web paywall targeted to the eligibility preset
    - [ ] SDK set up and the paywall displays for its trigger
    - [ ] Store purchase completes from the paywall
    - [ ] Web purchase completes and the app reopens with access unlocked ([go-live checklist](/guides/app2web-go-live-checklist))
    - [ ] Revenue webhooks arriving; [fallbacks](/guides/fallback-bundle) configured

    Start with a small audience and a 50/50 experiment of your current paywall vs. the App2Web version, then ramp up. See [Rolling out Helium](/guides/rolling-out-helium).
  </Step>
</Steps>

🎉 You now have a live Helium paywall. Questions along the way: your Helium Slack channel or [founders@tryhelium.com](mailto:founders@tryhelium.com).
