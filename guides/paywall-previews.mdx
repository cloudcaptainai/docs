Testing paywalls and entitlements is one of the most annoying parts of shipping an app — so we made it easy. Paywall Previews let you open any paywall you've made in Helium — published or draft — right on your device, without meeting production targeting or waiting for a release.

## Preview Paywalls from your App

**Requirements:** Integrate the Helium SDK into your app and navigate to any existing Helium paywall. You will need to have already set up a valid workflow.

Once you are viewing a paywall, **triple tap** anywhere on the paywall to bring up a list of all your paywalls — you can view the latest published version or latest draft version.

Triple tap again or dismiss the paywall to return to the list of paywalls.

## Good to know

- If a paywall has a second try offer configured, dismissing the preview shows the second try paywall with its own products and prices. (Second try is not supported on App2Web paywalls.)
- Web paywalls can't be triggered directly — they only back an App2Web paywall — but you can still preview any web paywall from here: it opens in the browser after a confirmation, for a **display-only** look. Purchases don't work there; to test the full checkout flow, use [App2Web previews](/guides/app2web-previews).

## Preview App2Web Paywalls

Previewing an App2Web paywall runs the full app-to-web checkout flow, including simulated or real purchases. See [App2Web Paywall Previews](/guides/app2web-previews).
