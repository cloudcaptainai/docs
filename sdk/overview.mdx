The Helium SDK fetches your paywalls when the app launches, presents the right one when your code fires a trigger, and reports purchases and paywall events back to Helium. Paywall design, targeting and experiments live in the [dashboard](https://app.tryhelium.com), so you ship the SDK once and change paywalls without app updates.

## What every integration does

1. **Initialize** Helium on app launch with your API key and a delegate for purchases.
2. **Present** a paywall for a trigger name at the point in your app where the paywall should appear.
3. **Ship fallbacks** so a paywall shows even if the device is offline on first launch. See [Fallback paywalls](/guides/fallback-bundle).
4. **Report revenue** by connecting your store or RevenueCat webhooks. See [Revenue reporting](/guides/revenue-reporting).

The quickstart for each platform walks through all four steps with the exact method names for that platform.
