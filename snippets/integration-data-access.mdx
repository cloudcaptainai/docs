<Accordion title="Recommended: give your agent access to detailed Helium logs" icon="database">
  1. Have an organization Admin open [Settings → Data Access](https://app.tryhelium.com/profile) and **Create secret key**. If the section is missing, ask your Helium contact to enable it.
  2. Give your coding agent the [Data Access skill](/data-access/agent-skill) and follow the [setup instructions](/data-access/overview#validate-your-integration) to connect it with your secret key.
  3. Your agent can now inspect detailed Helium events and errors to troubleshoot your integration and help you get live faster.
</Accordion>
